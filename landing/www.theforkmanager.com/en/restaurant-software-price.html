FAQs

Got questions? Reach out or explore our FAQs to get all the answers you need before opening a new restaurant.
Why should I use restaurant management software when opening a new restaurant?
Opening a new restaurant is exciting, but managing everything at once can be overwhelming. A restaurant management software for new restaurants like TheFork Manager simplifies operations by automating bookings, tracking guest preferences, and helping you manage tables efficiently. This means less time spent on admin tasks and more time focused on delivering a great dining experience.
What if my restaurant is a startup? How does a restaurant reservation solution for startups help me attract diners?
A restaurant reservation solution for startups connects you with diners looking for new places to eat. By listing your restaurant on TheFork and TripAdvisor, you increase your visibility and attract more bookings—even during off-peak hours. Plus, automated confirmations and real-time availability updates help reduce no-shows and ensure smooth service.
Does TheFork offer assistance alongside its restaurant operations software?
When opening a new restaurant, you need more than just software—you need reliable support. TheFork Manager is an intuitive restaurant operations software for new establishments, along with expert guidance. From free onboarding training to 7-day customer support, we ensure you’re never alone in managing your restaurant’s bookings, guest relationships, and marketing efforts.
