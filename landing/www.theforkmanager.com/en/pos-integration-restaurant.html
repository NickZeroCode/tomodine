How TheFork Manager and your POS work together

Table sync from TheFork Manager to your POS

When a customer arrives and the booking is marked as ARRIVED or SEATED in TheFork Manager a new table is opened in the POS and the information synchronised between the two systems.

Data integration

Receipt details are linked to bookings and automatically synced with TheFork Manager, providing expense information and a complete history for your guests.

Fast, easy check out

Special offers, discounts, menus and Yums linked to the reservation are automatically displayed in the POS.

FAQs

Any questions? Get in touch with us or check out our FAQs.
Can TheFork Manager integrate with my existing POS system?
Absolutely. TheFork Manager is built to support seamless POS integration for restaurants, ensuring that bookings and orders flow smoothly between your systems. Whether you're using a major POS provider or a more niche solution, chances are we already integrate—or we’re working on it!
What are the benefits of POS integration for my restaurant?
POS integration restaurant solutions like TheFork Manager help you save time, reduce manual errors, and deliver a better guest experience. By syncing table availability and orders, you can optimise service, personalise interactions, and boost operational efficiency across the board.
What’s unique about the integration between TheFork Manager and POS systems?
In addition to the usual benefits, it makes the processing of guest loyalty points or Special Offers easier with the integration between TheFork Manager and your POS system, further improving your diner experience.
