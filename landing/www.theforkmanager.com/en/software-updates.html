TheFork Manager software updates: always improving, just for you!

We’re continuously improving TheFork Manager to ensure you have all the restaurant management software features you need to deliver excellent service to your guests. Our commitment to regular software updates guarantees you access to the latest tools and innovations.

TheFork Manager updates: our commitment to innovation

Continuous improvement

Our team of over 50 dedicated technology experts works to improve TheFork Manager, consistently delivering new software updates. On average, we release more than 60 new features per year to help optimise your restaurant operations.

Harnessing cutting-edge technology

At TheFork, we leverage the latest technology, including AI-powered solutions, to bring you the most advanced restaurant management software. We are committed to making restaurant operations simpler, smarter, and more efficient, using technology that delivers real-world results.

Built with you, for you

We place great emphasis on your feedback, collaborating with our restaurant partners to co-create new features that cater to your needs. Your input is key to the development of TheFork Manager, ensuring our software updates meet your expectations!

Improved reservation list printing

When you print the reservation list, you can now decide which fields to print, whether to print in landscape or portrait, and in what order the information is displayed, to ensure your team can work more efficiently. To do this, use the printer icon in the home screen.

Table view on app

You can now use the table view on the app, which gives you an even better overview of your service and restaurant. You can also still use the reservation list.

Download the app

All your data in one place

Your restaurant statistics, performance, promotions, and reviews can all be found in one place. You can also choose from performance goals and get personalised tips on how to achieve them. Also available in the app!

Discover now

Mandatory reservation fields

You can create additional fields such as allergy information and seating preference for the guest to complete when making an online reservation. Simply put, you can now decide which fields are mandatory to complete.

Discover now

Secured Google Bookings

You can now request diners to secure their Google Reserve bookings using a credit card...a great way to prevent no-shows!

Simply get in touch with your Account Manager to discuss this feature.

Elevate Your Diner Experience with Our New Chat Feature!

A game-changing feature that will simplify the way you connect with your diners.

When a diner adds a Special Request note to their online booking, you’ll be able to view and reply to their request via instant chat.

Configure Your Notifications

Keep It In The Family

We’ve upgraded our technology so that your booking widget (your website) will suggest alternative restaurants from within your restaurant group, when the diner's first choice is fully booked.
