FAQs

Any questions? Get in touch with us or check out our FAQs.
How can a restaurant loyalty management solution help my business?
A restaurant loyalty management solution is designed to help restaurants retain customers by tracking their preferences, rewarding repeat visits, and creating personalised experiences. With TheFork Manager, you can collect guest data, send targeted offers, and build long-term relationships that keep diners returning.
How do restaurant CRM solutions improve guest engagement?
Restaurant CRM solutions centralise diners data, allowing you to understand preferences, booking history, and spending habits. TheFork Manager enables you to segment your audience, send tailored promotions, and manage communication efficiently—helping you enhance engagement and increase repeat visits.
How does the Yums loyalty program support restaurant customer retention?
TheFork Manager features a powerful restaurant loyalty management system through the Yums loyalty program, designed to enhance customer retention. Diners earn Yums points every time they book through TheFork, which can later be redeemed for exclusive discounts. This system helps restaurants foster stronger relationships with their guests while driving repeat visits and long-term loyalty.
